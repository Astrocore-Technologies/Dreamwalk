# Scenic interface art

The shared interface uses two decorative, theme-specific original illustrations:

- `apps/web/public/images/dreamwalk-world-night.webp` (1672 × 941, 169,108 bytes)
- `apps/web/public/images/dreamwalk-world-dawn.webp` (1672 × 941, 194,736 bytes)

Created on 2026-11-03 with built-in image generation for this interface. The night illustration depicts a lavender moonlit sky, floating castle islands, waterfalls, foliage and small golden lights around an intentionally calm center. The dawn variant preserves the composition with pearlescent lavender/rose sky, creamy mist and sage foliage. Neither asset includes text, controls, people, third-party downloaded artwork or a screenshot of a proposed interface. The files were encoded as WebP without upscaling; generation does not imply exclusive copyright ownership.

The art is decorative and non-interactive. It stays behind the application, with distinct light/dark overlays and readable content surfaces; gameplay retains its own map-focused background. Authored story covers remain unchanged. Browser tests verify both theme assets decode and capture actual wide, standard desktop and mobile renders for visual review.
